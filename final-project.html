Asset Library

Our asset library is a OneDrive folder that currently categorizes our creations into 3 primary folders:

  • NMI CLient Assets - Assets tailored for NMI clients and collaborators (e.g., Rock Lobsters)
  • NMI-Specific Assets - Assets for the NMI and its events (e.g., NMIXpo)
  • TEDxUGA - Assets specifically for TEDxUGA

A separate folder, Test Assets, has been included for our personal use and contains some previous versions of our assets, failed attempts, and other test animations.


For our purposes, an "asset" may be one of two things:

  • A static image (SVG file)
  • An animation (folder consisting of many SVG files prefixed with "seq-")

Currently, most of our assets are animations, and all assets are downloadable. Click here to view our asset libary.
